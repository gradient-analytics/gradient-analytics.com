Data Science and Research

Analytical systems for complex biological and environmental problems.

Gradient designs and delivers end to end data science and research solutions for organizations ranging from small technical teams to large, established institutions. We work at the intersection of analytics, modeling, and domain science to turn complex data into durable, defensible insight.

Our projects span the full lifecycle of analytical work, including data ingestion and ETL pipelines, cloud based storage and access control, statistical and machine learning models, process based and spatial simulations, and clear technical communication that supports decision making, validation, and long term use.

What we work on:
  • Vegetation, crop, and ecosystem response to management, chemistry, and environment
  • Soil chemistry and DNA based microbiome data interpreted in a functional context
  • Climate, pest, and risk modeling across spatial and temporal scales
  • Experimental design, systems modeling, and technically rigorous analysis

Core capabilities

Field and Vegetation Analytics

Quantitative design and analysis of field, greenhouse, and controlled environment studies. We support multi site and multi year experiments, integrating physiological, agronomic, and vegetation data into models that emphasize effect size, uncertainty, and biological meaning.

Microbial and Soil Systems

Analysis and interpretation of soil chemistry and microbiome data, including DNA based outputs from sequencing and commercial platforms. We focus on linking belowground signals to vegetation performance, system function, and management outcomes using literature grounded and reproducible approaches.

Climate and Geospatial Modeling

Development of spatial and temporal models that integrate weather records, reanalysis data, and climate projections. This work supports forecasting, risk assessment, and scenario analysis from local trials to regional and continental scales.

Decision, Documentation, and Research Support

Long form analytical support that extends beyond modeling. This includes literature assessment, technical document preparation and review, data archiving and indexing, and development of transparent analytical records that can support internal decisions, external review, and peer reviewed publication.

From complex data to durable systems

Gradient specializes in projects where data are imperfect, heterogeneous, and collected across sites, time periods, and measurement systems. We design analytical architectures that integrate these components into coherent systems rather than one off analyses.

This work often involves building and maintaining pipelines for data ingestion, storage, and access, including API based workflows and cloud hosted infrastructure that allow analyses to evolve, scale, and persist over time.

From fine scale signals to long term forecasts

Our analyses routinely span multiple orders of magnitude in scale, from soil DNA and plot level measurements to landscapes, regions, and future climate scenarios.

By combining domain knowledge with rigorous modeling, we help partners connect detailed biological signals to broader patterns, forecasts, and strategic decisions that unfold over years rather than weeks.
